# Product Context: AI Technical Interview System

## Problem Statement

### Current Challenges
- **Manual Interview Bottleneck:** Recruiting analysts spend significant time conducting initial technical screenings
- **Inconsistent Evaluation:** Different interviewers may assess candidates differently, leading to subjective results
- **Scalability Issues:** Limited human resources constrain the number of interviews that can be conducted simultaneously
- **Time-to-Hire Delays:** Manual processes slow down the recruitment pipeline for client positions

### Target Users
1. **Recruiting Analysts:** Need efficient tools to manage and evaluate candidates for client positions
2. **System Administrators:** Require user management and system configuration capabilities
3. **Candidates (Globers):** Need accessible, fair, and standardized interview experiences

## Solution Vision

### How It Works
1. **Interview Setup:** Recruiting analysts configure interviews by associating candidates with specific Job Descriptions
2. **AI-Driven Assessment:** AI agent conducts conversational technical interviews based on role requirements
3. **Objective Evaluation:** System generates structured feedback focusing on quantifiable technical skills
4. **Streamlined Process:** Automated workflow from scheduling to feedback delivery

### Key Benefits
- **Efficiency:** Reduce manual interview time and administrative overhead
- **Consistency:** Standardized evaluation criteria across all technical assessments
- **Objectivity:** Focus on measurable technical competencies, eliminating subjective bias
- **Scalability:** Handle multiple interviews simultaneously without resource constraints
- **Quality:** Comprehensive feedback based on Job Description alignment

## User Experience Goals

### For Recruiting Analysts
- **Intuitive Interface:** Easy-to-use forms for interview configuration and candidate management
- **Comprehensive Oversight:** Clear visibility into interview status and candidate pipeline
- **Actionable Insights:** Structured feedback that supports hiring decisions
- **Efficient Workflow:** Streamlined process from candidate registration to final assessment

### For Candidates
- **Professional Experience:** Well-structured interview process that reflects positively on Globant
- **Fair Assessment:** Objective evaluation based on technical competencies
- **Clear Instructions:** Easy-to-understand interview access and process
- **Timely Feedback:** Prompt completion of assessment without revealing specific results

### For System Administrators
- **User Management:** Efficient tools for managing analyst and candidate accounts
- **System Configuration:** Easy maintenance of lookup tables and system settings
- **Data Integrity:** Reliable data management and security controls

## Business Impact

### Immediate Benefits
- Reduced time-to-hire for client positions
- Increased interview capacity without additional human resources
- Standardized technical assessment process
- Improved candidate experience consistency

### Long-term Value
- Enhanced recruitment quality through objective assessments
- Scalable foundation for handling increased interview volume
- Data-driven insights into candidate technical competencies
- Improved client satisfaction through faster, more reliable hiring processes

## Success Metrics
- **Efficiency:** Reduction in manual interview time per candidate
- **Consistency:** Standardized evaluation scores across similar roles
- **User Satisfaction:** Positive feedback from analysts and candidates
- **Process Reliability:** Successful completion rate of AI-conducted interviews
