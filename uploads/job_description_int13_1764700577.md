**About the Role**  
We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to join our data engineering team and help build scalable, secure, and high-performance data infrastructure. You will work with modern data platforms and cloud services to support analytics, machine learning, and business intelligence initiatives across the organization.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines using Databricks, Snowflake, Apache Spark, Python, and Airflow.  
* Leverage AWS services (e.g., S3, Lambda, Glue, EMR, KMS) to manage data storage, processing, and security.  
* Write efficient, maintainable code in Python and SQL for data transformation and automation.  
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams including data scientists, analysts, and software engineers.  
* Ensure data quality, governance, and compliance with security standards.  
* Monitor and troubleshoot data workflows to ensure reliability and performance.  
* Work independently and demonstrate self-motivation in a fast-paced environment.  
* Communicate effectively with team members and stakeholders.

**Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in data engineering or a related field.  
* Strong hands-on experience with Databricks, Snowflake, Airflow, AWS, Python, SQL, and Apache Spark.  
* Solid understanding of data warehousing, distributed systems, and cloud-native architectures.  
* Experience with CI/CD pipelines and version control (e.g., Git).  
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.  
* Excellent communication and collaboration abilities.  
* Ability to work independently and demonstrate self-motivation.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience with Scala and Java.  
* Familiarity with Trino (PrestoSQL) for federated querying.  
* Exposure to Apache Flink for real-time stream processing.
